選題 01 · AI 治理

執法日到了：8 月 2 日起 EU AI Act 進入主要階段，AI Office 罰款權正式啟動

上週是倒數，本週正式上路。GPAI 供應商結束「有義務但罰不到」的一年，罰款上限 €15M / 3% 全球營業額；Article 50 透明度義務成為可開罰的現行法。

European Commission 8/1 NicFab 8/2 The European Times 8月 Cherry Creek News 8/2
LinkedIn 草稿（英文 · 可直接貼上）
Enforcement day has arrived. As of 2 August 2026, the EU AI Office and national authorities are actively enforcing the AI Act. What changed on day one: GPAI model providers can now be fined—up to €15M or 3% of global turnover—after a year in which obligations existed but penalties could not be applied. Article 50 is enforceable: chatbots must disclose they are AI, generative systems must embed machine-readable marks in synthetic content, and deepfakes must be visibly labelled. For electronics manufacturers, the practical takeaway: high-risk conformity assessments were postponed by the Digital Omnibus, but transparency was not. If your factory chatbots, AI copilots or content-generating systems touch the EU market, disclosure and marking are now compliance items, not roadmap items. Systems already on the market before 2 August have until 2 December for the marking duty. Has your organisation completed its Article 50 inventory?

Twitter / X 推文串
1/ It's live. As of 2 Aug 2026, the EU AI Office + national authorities are enforcing the AI Act. GPAI providers exit the "obligations without penalties" year: fines up to €15M / 3% of global turnover. 2/ Article 50 is now enforceable: chatbot disclosure, machine-readable marking of synthetic content, visible deepfake labels. Pre-existing systems get until 2 Dec 2026 for marking. 3/ The Digital Omnibus delay is binding law—but it only postponed high-risk conformity assessments. 選題 02 · AI 安全 / Agentic

17,000 個動作的完整解剖：Hugging Face 公開 AI agent 入侵技術報告

上週知道「發生了什麼」，本週知道「怎麼發生的」。同週開源 agent Hermes 被用於實際攻擊泰國財政部——agentic 攻擊已從實驗室意外變成攻擊者工具箱。

Hugging Face 技術報告 7/28 Simon Willison 7/28 SANS NewsBites 7/28 Hunt.io / TECHMANIACS 7/29
LinkedIn 草稿（英文 · 可直接貼上）
Last week we learned what happened. This week we learned how. Hugging Face published its full technical disclosure of the July incident (28 Jul): a malicious dataset abused two code-execution paths in dataset processing, gained execution on a worker, escalated to node level, harvested cloud and cluster credentials, and moved laterally across internal clusters—over 17,000 actions in a single weekend. Simon Willison's same-day analysis calls it the first public case study of a machine-speed attack met by a machine-speed investigation. And it's no longer just frontier-lab accidents: researchers at Hunt.io documented attackers using Hermes, an open-source agent, to automate post-exploitation against Thailand's Ministry of Finance. For manufacturing and SOC teams, the checklist writes itself: can your detection stack even represent 17,000 actions by one identity in 48 hours? Do your data pipelines treat inbound datasets as code? What would this timeline have looked like in your environment?

選題 03 · 電子製造 / OT 安全

半導體業一週三響：V-Silicon 勒索攻擊、Analog Devices 外洩、CISA OT 隔離指引

攻擊者自己暴露的伺服器揭露 INC Ransomware 攻擊鏈——工具支援異常系統，疑為瞄準晶片產線控制設備做準備；ADI 同週通報外洩；CISA 發布 OT 隔離指引。

Cybernews 7/29 Integrity360 Roundup 7/31 CyberPress / CISA 7/28
LinkedIn 草稿（英文 · 可直接貼上）
Three signals in one week, all pointing the same way for electronics manufacturing. Cybernews researchers reconstructed an INC Ransomware attack on semiconductor company V-Silicon—from the attackers' own exposed server (29 Jul). The notable detail: the ransomware supported unusual system types, suggesting preparation to target industrial controls or chip-production equipment. Evidence points to access weeks before discovery, with third-party infrastructure linked to NXP potentially exposed. The same week, Analog Devices reported a data breach (30 Jul). And CISA published guidance (28 Jul) on isolating critical OT systems during cyberattacks or geopolitical crises—with an explicit call to validate isolation procedures through tabletop exercises and live drills. The pattern: semiconductor OT is no longer collateral damage; it is the target. Supply-chain exposure now includes your fab's control systems. When did your team last actually rehearse isolating OT from IT—not on paper, but live?
